# Apple App Review 備註草稿

## Review Notes（貼給審核員，英文）

Rail Island World is a self-contained interactive visualization for five city rail networks: Tokyo, New York, London, Istanbul, and Singapore.

Train markers are simulations derived from public timetables or stated service headways. They are not live vehicle locations and must not be used for travel decisions. The rail geometry and timetable/headway data are bundled with the app. An internet connection is used only for the OpenFreeMap vector basemap and user-initiated public links.

No account, sign-in, subscription, purchase, advertising, location, push notification, analytics, or demo credentials are required. All primary features are available immediately after the first-use guide.

Suggested review path:
1. Launch the app and dismiss the first-use guide.
2. Use the city selector at the top to switch among the five cities.
3. Tap a station to view upcoming simulated services.
4. Tap a moving train marker to follow it. Route/service labels become visible when zoomed in or following.
5. Open the information panel to switch among Traditional Chinese, English, and Japanese and to access Privacy, Terms, and Support.
6. Use Share to open the native iOS share sheet for the current public map link.

This is not a thin web bookmark. It bundles the selected cities’ rail/timetable data, renders a full-screen animated canvas map, keeps preferences on device, follows simulated trains, presents station service views in local city time, and integrates the native share sheet. It is distinct from the Taiwan-focused Rail Island app: that app covers Taiwan and local live-delay features, while this app covers the five international cities above and makes no live-position claim.

Support and About contain one unobtrusive, platform-appropriate link to the already released Taiwan-focused app. It does not appear as a launch prompt, banner, advertisement, or requirement for using Rail Island World.

The map retains OpenFreeMap / OpenMapTiles / OpenStreetMap attribution. Source scope and limitations are available inside the app.
