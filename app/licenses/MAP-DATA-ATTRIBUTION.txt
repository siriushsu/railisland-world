Online basemap and map data attribution

OpenFreeMap — https://openfreemap.org/
OpenMapTiles — https://openmaptiles.org/
Data from OpenStreetMap contributors — https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

OpenStreetMap data is made available under the Open Data Commons Open Database
License (ODbL): https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

OpenFreeMap's hosted service is used to deliver the online vector basemap.
Attribution remains visible on the map. Rail routes and timetable/headway data
are separate from the basemap; each bundled data/*.json file contains its own
source_notes field with source and scope information.
